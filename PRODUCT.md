# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Career mode players who want a fresh save idea before starting EA FC or FIFA. They are usually on a phone, between matches or while sitting at the console, and want a fast recommendation that gives a club, rules, and a story hook without needing a spreadsheet.

## Product Purpose

Career Mode Lab turns a large multi-country club pool into guided save ideas. It helps the user choose or randomize a club, apply constraints, draw challenges, and keep a compact save brief that makes the career mode feel less repetitive.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, sharp, and playful. The product should feel like a matchday notebook crossed with a scouting desk: focused enough to trust, lively enough to make a save feel dramatic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic fantasy football dashboards, neon gamer UI, purple AI gradients, fake betting-style stat panels, and identical card grids. The app should not feel like a marketing landing page wrapped around a future tool.

## Design Principles

- Make the first useful action immediate: pick filters, randomize, and get a save brief.
- Give every recommendation a story, not just a club name.
- Treat crests and league identity as core content, not decoration.
- Keep mobile controls thumb-friendly and dense enough for repeated rerolls.
- Make challenge rules readable while the user is setting up a console save.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, no color-only state communication, and reduced-motion support for scroll and reveal effects.
